# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-07-19*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** MoneyCompass is a Lithuanian-first personal finance web app that turns manually uploaded bank statements into a clear view of spending, balances, net worth, investments and goals without connecting a bank account.

**What it does:** Users export a CSV or Excel statement from their bank, upload it themselves, review and categorize transactions, then use dashboards and reports to understand their finances. The product also supports budgets, savings goals, assets and portfolio tracking.

**Product category:** Personal finance management software / expense tracker.

**Business model:** Open beta. The first beta users receive 365 days of access at no charge. Post-beta pricing and packaging are not yet confirmed.

## Target Audience

**Primary customer profile:** Lithuanian adults who want a clearer view of personal finances and prefer not to connect a third-party app directly to their bank account.

**Target companies:** Not applicable; this is a direct-to-consumer product.

**Decision-maker:** The individual user decides whether to register and use the product.

**Users and buyers:** The user and buyer are the same individual consumer. Early beta users are comfortable exporting a bank statement and want more control than a bank's basic transaction view provides.

**Primary jobs to be done:**
- Understand where money goes each month.
- See spending, balances, assets and goals in one place.
- Analyze bank transactions without sharing bank login credentials.
- Build a repeatable personal-finance review habit.

**Primary use cases:** Monthly spending review, category cleanup, multi-account overview, budget monitoring, net-worth review and goal tracking.

**Pain points:** Transactions are spread across banks and files; bank interfaces show activity but not a complete financial picture; manual spreadsheets take time; direct bank connections can feel intrusive.

## Personas

The consumer is the user, champion and decision maker.

| Persona | Cares about | Barrier | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| Privacy-conscious planner | Control and a complete overview | Does not want bank syncing | Import the statement yourself; no bank connection |
| Budget starter | Simple categories and spending clarity | Finance tools feel complicated | Start with one statement and see where money goes |
| Multi-account tracker | A unified picture of money and assets | Data lives in several places | Bring supported statements into one private overview |

## Positioning

**Positioning statement:** For people in Lithuania who want to understand their money without connecting a bank account, MoneyCompass is a personal finance app that converts user-controlled statement imports into useful spending and net-worth views. Unlike bank-only views or a hand-built spreadsheet, it brings transactions, assets, investments and goals together while keeping import control with the user.

**Value pillars:**
1. **Clarity:** Categories, trends, dashboards and goals turn transaction rows into a useful picture.
2. **User control:** The user decides which statement to export and upload; MoneyCompass does not log in to the bank.
3. **One financial view:** Spending, balances, assets, investments and goals can be reviewed together.
4. **Lithuanian-first:** Product and educational content are designed first for Lithuanian users, with English support.

**Product capabilities verified in the application:** CSV/Excel statement import, transaction categorization, spending statistics, budget limits, goals, asset/net-worth views, portfolio tracking, Lithuanian and English interfaces.

**Differentiation:** User-controlled statement import and broad personal-finance visibility. Competitor names and comparative performance claims require separate research before publication.

## Messaging

**Primary promise:** Pagaliau aišku, kur dingsta tavo pinigai.

**Supporting message:** Įkelk banko išrašą ir vienoje vietoje matyk išlaidas, balansą, turtą, investicijas bei tikslus. Be banko paskyros prijungimo.

**Proof points available now:** Working application screens and documented product behavior. There are no approved customer testimonials or independently verified savings outcomes yet.

**Objections and responses:**
- “Will it connect to my bank?” — No. The user exports and uploads a supported CSV/Excel statement.
- “Is it financial advice?” — No. It organizes and visualizes user data; the user makes financial decisions.
- “Is beta paid?” — The current beta offer grants 365 days at no charge to eligible beta users.

**Words and claims to avoid:** automatic bank sync, bank-grade security, guaranteed savings, financial adviser, every Lithuanian bank, instant import, or any unverified numerical performance claim.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Clear, calm, practical and trustworthy.

**Personality:** A patient financial organizer, not a guru or adviser.

**Style:** Use plain Lithuanian, short sentences and specific product behavior. Avoid fear tactics, hype, condescension and promises of wealth.

## Funnel and Goals

**Primary acquisition goal:** Qualified beta registrations.

**Primary conversion:** Completed account registration (`sign_up` in GA4 and `CompleteRegistration` in Meta when consent permits).

**Activation path:** Visit focused beta page → register → activate account by email → import first statement → review categorized transactions/dashboard.

**Channels in scope:** Lithuanian organic search, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Meta paid social and later TikTok paid/organic expansion.

**Measurement:** Standard UTM links, first-touch attribution stored on the user profile, GA4 Consent Mode, Meta Pixel behind explicit marketing consent.

## SEO and Discoverability

**Primary themes:** asmeniniai finansai, asmeninis biudžetas, finansų programėlė, išlaidų sekimas, banko išrašo analizė, finansų planavimas.

**Search intent:** Educational articles should solve a specific financial-management question and link naturally to the relevant MoneyCompass workflow. Product pages should describe what the app does and who it is for, not repeat keywords unnaturally.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** People can see individual transactions but struggle to turn them into a clear, unified understanding of spending and financial progress.

**Why alternatives fall short:** Bank views are limited to that institution; spreadsheets require ongoing manual maintenance; some finance apps require a direct bank connection that privacy-conscious users do not want.

**What it costs:** Time spent merging data, missed spending patterns, and less confidence in monthly financial decisions. No monetary outcome has been independently measured yet.

**Emotional tension:** Users want clarity and control, but often feel that a complete view requires either giving up privacy or maintaining a complex spreadsheet.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct competitors:** Personal-finance and expense-tracking apps. Specific competitor names and feature comparisons require current market research before publication.

**Secondary alternatives:** Bank transaction views, spreadsheets, notes and doing nothing.

## Differentiation

**Primary differentiator:** MoneyCompass combines user-controlled CSV/Excel statement import with spending, assets, investments and goals in one Lithuanian-first experience.

**What we do differently:** The product does not require direct bank login or bank syncing. The user decides what to export and upload.

**Why customers choose us:** They want more clarity than a bank view and more convenience than a hand-built spreadsheet while retaining control over the import process. This intended reason is not yet validated by customer research.

## Objections

**Common objections and responses:** Bank connection is not required; MoneyCompass is not financial advice; eligible beta users receive 365 days at no charge.

**Anti-persona:** Someone who requires real-time automatic bank syncing and is unwilling to export a statement is not a good fit for the current product.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Fragmented bank views and time-consuming spreadsheets.

**Pull:** A clearer Lithuanian-first overview with user-controlled imports.

**Habit:** Continuing to review finances only inside each bank or postponing the task.

**Anxiety:** Import effort, data privacy and uncertainty about supported file formats.
